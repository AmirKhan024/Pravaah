What Pravaah is, in one paragraph

A deterministic crowd-flow simulator for event organisers. It rehearses a full evening minute-by-minute (hotels, trains, roads, gates in one model), predicts where and when a crowd crush happens via a 60-run ensemble, explains the cause by removing factors and re-running, proves a fix by simulating every candidate plan (including the ones that fail), and hands the approved plan to real people: crowd messages in three languages, a live "Room" where phones vote and the simulation re-runs with real acceptance rates, a stress-test against 192 bad nights, and a tamper-evident log of every decision. No LLM ever produces a number — an engine (simulate(), ported faithfully from a working prototype with a golden test) produces every figure on screen; an LLM only rewords text and parses typed what-if questions.

Known limitations (we already know these)

Models arrival only, not egress (leaving the venue). Hotel/lane/lane-rate numbers for DY Patil are plausible estimates, not surveyed. Nudge-acceptance parameters are hand-calibrated (The Room is a live check on them). The Room's server state is in-memory on the demo laptop, not persisted. The Bengaluru replay deliberately omits gate-lane plans because the engine doesn't cap entry at venue capacity. SMS delivery needs Twilio keys we don't have. Tell us if you find more.
